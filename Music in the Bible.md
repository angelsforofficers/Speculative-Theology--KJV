Oshun is a benevolent Orisha (deity) in the Yoruba religion who presides over love, beauty, wealth, sensuality, fertility, and sweet waters. Originating in West Africa, her worship also spread to the Americas through the African diaspora, where she is known by variations like Oxum (Candomblé) and Ochun (Santeria). Depicted as a beautiful, sensual woman adorned in gold, she is associated with rivers, the color yellow, mirrors, and honey, and is a patron of women and those seeking blessings in love and abundance.
